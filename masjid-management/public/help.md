# Getting started

## What the Masjid Rahma app is
The Masjid Rahma app keeps the masjid's food-duty rotation (which home gives food on which day), the members (homes) and their dues, the masjid's income and expenses, bank accounts, announcements and notifications.
Anyone can open the app without logging in and see today's food duty, the yearly schedule and About the masjid. Members log in to see their own dues, payments and duty days. The admin manages everything.

## Logging in
Tap Login at the top right (on a phone, the Log in tab at the bottom). Type your Member ID (like MR#012) and your password, then tap Log In.
A member's first password is the phone number on their member record. Change it to your own in Settings > Change password.
You stay logged in for 30 days.
To log out, tap your photo at the top right > Logout.

## Forgot your password
If you never changed it, your password is the phone number on your member record. If you changed it and forgot it, ask the masjid admin to reset it: then your phone number is your password again, and you can set a new one in Settings > Change password.

## The first admin account
On a new app with no admin yet, the button at the top says Set Up Admin Account. The first account made there is the admin that manages the masjid.

# Where things are

## The menu
On a computer the menu is on the left: Dashboard, Transactions and Banks (admin), Profile and Members (logged in), Yearly Schedule, Assistant, About, Activity (admin), Settings (logged in).
On a phone the tabs at the bottom are Home, Schedule, Assistant, then Accounts (admin), Profile (member) or Log in (not logged in), and More, which opens the rest of the menu.
Tap your photo at the top right for My Notifications (members), Announcements and Logout. A red number on it means unread notifications or announcements.
On a computer the Ask Assistant button at the bottom right opens the Assistant.

## Using the app on your phone
Open the app in the phone's browser and add it to the Home Screen (iPhone: Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen; Android: browser menu > Install app or Add to Home screen). On iPhone this is needed for notifications.

# Food duty

## Today's food duty
The card at the top of the home page ("ഇന്നത്തെ ഭക്ഷണം") shows the home giving food today, with their phone number (tap it to call). "മാറ്റം വരുത്തി" means today was swapped, and "യഥാർത്ഥത്തിൽ" shows the home that was first on the schedule. "നിങ്ങളുടെ ഊഴം!" means it is your turn today.
Logged-in members also see their own next turn on the card; the admin sees tomorrow's home.
Tap the card to open the Yearly Schedule.

## The yearly schedule
Yearly Schedule (Schedule tab on a phone) shows every home's food-duty days, month by month. Use the arrows to see the previous or next year. Swapped days are marked "Swapped away" or "Swapped in" - point at or tap a day to see the details.
Only Regular members are in the food rotation; Non-Rotation members have no food duty.

## Set a reminder for your turn
Roles: member
On the home page, tap your next turn ("നിങ്ങളുടെ ഊഴം") on the food-duty card. Check the subject and date, then tap "Google കലണ്ടറിൽ ചേർക്കുക" (add to Google Calendar) or ".ics ഡൗൺലോഡ് ചെയ്യുക" (download a calendar file for any calendar app). Your calendar app then gives the reminder.
You can also get a push notification the day before your turn: Settings > Notifications > Food-day reminders.

## Swap a food-duty day
Roles: admin
On the home page, tap the swap button on the food-duty card. Choose:
One-time swap for a day: pick the date and the home that gives food that day.
Swap two dates with each other: pick the first and second date; the two homes change days.
Tap Save. To undo today's swap, tap the undo button on the card.

## Add or remove a home from the rotation
Roles: admin
Members > edit a member > Food Supply Rotation: Regular (included in food rotation) or Non-Rotation (no food duty). Turning off Active in Rotation also takes the home out of the rotation.

# Members

## The members list
Members shows every home with its Member ID, name and phone. Search by name, member ID, address or phone, and filter All, Regular or Non-rotation. The ring around a photo shows whether that member has notifications turned on.

## Add a member
Roles: admin
Members > Add member. Fill in Home / Family Name, Address, Phone Number, Number of Members in Home, the Recurring Payment Amount and Payment Frequency (Monthly or Yearly), Food Supply Rotation, and Active in Rotation. Save.
The app gives the member a Member ID (MR#...). The member logs in with that ID and the phone number as the first password.

## Edit a member, their dues or photo
Roles: admin
Members > the edit button next to the member. Change any detail and save. Change photo / Remove photo sets the picture shown for the member.
Changing the phone number also changes a member's login password, unless they set their own password.
Reset password (under the photo, then Reset) is for a member who forgot their own password: they log in with the phone number on their record again. The member needs a phone number on their record.
Dues Start Month (monthly) or Dues Start Year (yearly): if a member only started paying from a later month or year, set it here so the earlier months show as Nil instead of Missed. Leave it blank to count every year from January.

# Dues and the profile

## Your profile
Roles: member, admin
Profile (on a phone the Profile tab; the home page shows it too) shows a member's details, Total expected, Total paid and Pending dues (or Extra paid), each year's monthly payments and the transactions.
A member sees only their own profile. The admin picks any member in "Select a member".

## What the month statuses mean
Paid: the month is paid.
Partial: part of the month is paid.
Missed: the month has started and is not paid.
Nil: nothing owed - a future month, or before the member's dues start.
Payments fill the months in order from January. Each calendar year is counted on its own. Use the arrows next to "<year> monthly payments" to see other years.

## How dues are paid
Roles: member, admin
Dues are paid to the masjid and the admin records them as a Masjid payment for the member. The member then gets a notification, and it shows on their profile.

# Transactions
Roles: admin

## Add a transaction
Open Transactions in the menu (on a phone, the Accounts tab at the bottom), then tap Add transaction. Choose the Transaction Type (Income or Expense), the Category, the Bank (optional), the Member, the Amount, a Description (optional) and the Transaction Date. Save.
Income categories: Masjid payment, Jummah Collection, General Income, Donation, Zakat, Masjid Fund, Other Income, Collection Drive, General, Nikah, Opening Balance, Sadaqah.
Expense categories: Imam Salary, Staff Salary, Utilities, Maintenance, Supplies, Staff, Events, Miscellaneous, General, Industrial Work Salary, Industrial Materials, Tax.
A member is needed for a Masjid payment (a member's dues). For other entries it is optional; choosing a member notifies that member of the entry.

## Edit, delete or print a receipt
In the Transactions list use the buttons on a row (on a phone, the ... button): Print receipt (income only), Edit, Delete. Tick several rows to delete them together.
The receipt shows the receipt number, date, who it was received from, the category, description and amount.

## Find transactions
Search by category or description, and filter by Income or Expense, Category and Member.

## Export all transactions
Settings > Export data > Export as CSV or Export as JSON downloads every transaction.

# Dashboard and money
Roles: admin

## The dashboard
The admin's Dashboard shows the Overview (all time): Total Income, Total Expenses, Current Balance, Total Masjid Payment, Staff Salary and Members. Tap a card for details: Income and Expenses open their transactions, Current Balance shows each bank, Staff Salary and Members show a breakdown - tap a part of the chart to see those transactions or members.
Money shows This year, Last 12 months or All time: month by month in and out, where the money goes and where it comes from. Tap a category to see its transactions.

## Banks
Banks shows where the masjid's money is, each account's balance and All accounts together. Tap an account to see its entries. Add bank: a Name and the Opening balance. The pencil button edits an account.
Choose the bank on a transaction so it counts in that account's balance.

# Announcements and notifications

## Read announcements
Roles: member, admin
Tap your photo at the top right > Announcements (on a member's home page, the Announcements button). It shows the news the masjid sent to all members.

## Send an announcement
Roles: admin
Announcements > Send an announcement: type the Title and the Message, tap Send to all members and confirm. It goes to every member who has notifications turned on, and stays in Recent announcements. Delete one with its delete button, or clear them all.

## Remind members with pending payments
Roles: admin
Announcements > Pending masjid payments lists members who owe, with their plan, the amount due and the missed months. Tap the bell next to a member to remind them, or Remind all. Only members with notifications turned on can get a reminder.

## My notifications
Roles: member
Tap your photo > My Notifications (or Notifications on the home page): payment reminders and messages sent to you, such as a payment recorded for you. Delete one or clear them all.

## Turn on notifications
Roles: member
Settings > Notifications > Food-day reminders: allow notifications when the phone asks. On iPhone add the app to the Home Screen first. You then get a notification the day before your food-duty turn, and announcements and payment messages.

# Settings

## Theme and contacts
Roles: member, admin
Settings > Appearance > Theme: Light or Dark.
Settings > Who to call lists the masjid committee, the Muaddin and who to ask about this app, with phone numbers.

## Change your password
Roles: member, admin
Settings > Change password: type your current password (if you never changed it, it is your phone number), the new password twice, and save.

## The masjid's settings
Roles: admin
Settings > The masjid has: Currency (used for all amounts), About the masjid (tagline, founded, address, story and the people - shown to everyone on the home page and under About), Assistant (questions per day for each member, visitors and the admin; 0 = no limit), Telegram (the bot), Claude access (MCP) and Export data. The edit button on a Who to call contact changes its name or phone.

## Activity
Roles: admin
Activity shows who used the app, when and from where: log ins, log outs and page visits, with the device and location. Filter by type, delete selected events or clear the log.

# About the masjid

## About
About (in the menu, and on the home page before logging in) shows the masjid's story, who is serving the masjid today and the founders. The admin edits it in Settings > About the masjid.

# The Assistant

## What the Assistant does
The Assistant answers questions about the masjid in Malayalam, English or another language. Without logging in it answers only about the food-duty schedule. A member can also ask about their own dues, payments, duty days and announcements. The admin can ask about any member, dues, income and expenses, and charts over time.
It only reads the records - it cannot change anything. Check important figures on the Profile or Dashboard.

## Ask a question
Open Assistant from the menu (on a phone the Assistant tab; on a computer Ask Assistant at the bottom right). Type, or tap a ready question. Your recent questions are there to ask again. Ask for a table or a chart and the answer comes as one.
Under an answer: Copy, Save as image and Print.
The number at the top is how many questions are left today. The clear button starts a new chat.

## Speak your question
Tap the microphone, speak, then tap the tick (the x cancels). Choose the language next to Voice. With Auto-send on, the question is sent as soon as it is turned into text.

# Telegram

## Ask the Assistant on Telegram
The same Assistant answers on Telegram, with the same daily limits. Without linking, it answers as a guest - only the food-duty schedule. Voice messages work too.

## Link or unlink your Telegram
Roles: member
Link it: Profile > Telegram > Link my Telegram > Open Telegram, then press Start (the link works once, within 10 minutes). Or open the masjid bot and share your phone number - the number on your member record.
To unlink: Profile > Telegram > the red unlink button. The bot then answers that account as a guest.

## Set up the bot
Roles: admin
Settings > Telegram shows whether the bot is connected and how many accounts are linked. If Telegram does not send messages here, tap Connect the bot.

## Claude access (MCP)
Roles: admin
Settings > Claude access (MCP) makes a personal token so Claude Code on your computer can read the masjid records. Type a name and create it, then run the command it shows in a terminal on that computer. Revoke a token to stop it at once.
